# Pricing decision governance

`PRC-DEC-01` turns the existing pricing analysis into a reviewable operating workflow. It deliberately stops before approval or ERP publication.

The governed sequence is:

1. **Prepare:** one proposal per product, including no-change and return-for-data outcomes.
2. **Route:** assign the delegated reviewer from the action and magnitude of the proposed change.
3. **Review:** keep every material human decision open in the public demonstration.
4. **Monitor:** compare an approved historical list-price change with invoiced and pocket-price movement over fixed pre/post windows.
5. **Return or rollback:** send weak evidence, missing cost data, poor realization or excessive giveback back to an accountable owner.

The realization measure is observational. Customer mix, concurrent discounts, contracts, competitor moves and seasonality can change pocket price after approval. The monitor therefore identifies where an explanation or controlled test is needed; it does not attribute a causal effect to the price decision.

All committed data is synthetic and reproducible. No file is a customer price list, an approval record or evidence of realized production benefit.
